# Spreadwatcher – cTrader Research Tool

Personal application for quantitative trading research, historical market data analysis,
backtesting and integration with the cTrader Open API.

Spreadwatcher monitors spreads, analyses market data and accesses the owner's account
information via the cTrader Open API. It records bid and ask prices of the owner's own trading
accounts and compares spreads between brokers and accounts. It may also process trading signals
and execute trades on the owner's own accounts, only if the owner has granted trading permission
and enabled this. cTrader accounts are connected only after explicit authorisation through the
official cTrader OAuth process. Spreadwatcher is not a broker and gives no investment advice.
